# Vulqen

Vulqen is a small glass companion that lives in a corner of your Windows desktop. She holds today's list, runs your focus timer, cheers when you get things done and reacts when a distracting site comes up during a focus.

## Get Vulqen

- **Windows 10 and 11**: [download Vulqen-Setup.exe](https://github.com/Dastinio28/vulqen/releases/latest/download/Vulqen-Setup.exe)
- **Phone or any browser**: [vulqen.web.app](https://vulqen.web.app)

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because Vulqen is new and not signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Using her

- Click Vulqen to open her menu: Today, Focus, Sleep, App.
- Drag her anywhere, or give her a throw.
- Click her icon again (Start menu, desktop or tray) to open the Vulqen app: focus length, her size and color, what she does, and your week.
- In the app, **Phone and sharing** shows a code: scan it with your phone to get the same list and focus timer there.

## Privacy

Window titles are read on your PC to spot distracting sites and never leave it. Phone sync stays off until you turn it on, and then shares only your tasks, focus length, the focus timer and focus totals.

_Vulqen was called Veyra until version 0.6. Old links and installs keep working and update on their own._
